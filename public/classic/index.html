About Me

I am a third-year undergraduate at the Indian Institute of Technology, Kanpur, pursuing a B.S. in Mathematics and Scientific Computing. I am currently pursuing research with Prof. Sayak Ray Chowdhury from Department of Computer Science and Engineering at IITK. My primary research interests include:

  • Alignment in Large Language Models: I have worked on Test-Time Alignment and Optimization in LLMs, I am also working on studying underlying geometry of hidden states in LLMs and its relation to factuality, hallucination, safety and robustness.
  • Reinforcement Learning & Robotics: I just love to play with robots. There is no grand philosophical motivation here... I just enjoy treating RL as highly complex toys to tinker with.

Alongside these I have also worked with Computational Neuroscience, where generally I am interested in SNNs and their biological plausibility.
