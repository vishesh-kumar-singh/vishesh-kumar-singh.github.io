Today is October 17 2026, I am travelling from Kanpur to Varanasi in New Delhi Vande Bharat Express, coach number C14. Does it ring a bell?? Ohh you forgot so soon? It has been just about a year since that day. Okay, I will tell you but if we are going into the past, why don't we start from the very beginning.

You remember na how our first meet was... Or shall I go more back? Hmm... that online class would be perfect to start. It was early January 2022, I joined an online class of a coaching center, I was recently admitted to. And it was a lecture on electricity. And me being a single guy, was it possible I would not look at what girls were there, definitely, all I could see were only names of people. And the only name I liked was you. Definitely I was a nerdy type guy too... I had to answer the questions first, no one came close to me in solving circuits, ohh, but there was one more voice answering the questions. I was still always first, but you maintained your second position. Ohh and then like every desperate guy, I just had to talk to you anyhow, and guess what, one day I got a text, and it was you... Ohh I was like damn, does she like me or something, she also wanted just a reason to talk to me? It was a doubt, but we talked about half an hour or so after that. And then, blaaaaankkk... no text from my side, none from yours, I wanted to talk, but over what?? Didn't wanna creep you out.

Then came February 2022, the new session for academic year 2022-23 started. But all the time, I just wanted to know who you actually were... I never saw you until then... a few days after everything started, I still hadn't found out who you were, I was getting restless, I was sitting in some of the initial benches on the right side, girls sat on the left, suddenly, a voice hit my ears... Someone just called out your name just for asking for water, faster than a cat reflex, I just bent my neck, and then there you were, passing the bottle of water. The person I liked just by talking to her for 30 minutes over WhatsApp, was sitting just in front of me, an angel, right before my eyes, laughing, talking to her friends. But how do I start talking to you now? If I just close my eyes, I can still see you getting those problem sheets from the office. It was you and your best friend ofcourse. I was just too desperate to start a conversation, that I finally asked you... Are you her... Hmm, but I confirmed your name and you didn't even ask mine... Your friend did ofcourse. Then I got to talk to you. I got your Telegram ID from the Telegram group, and definitely the best way to start a conversation. Ranting over teachers, doubts, notes, honestly I believe we both knew what we were doing and definitely we both didn't care about the notes ofcourse. But we talked, because we wanted to... Definitely I flirted... but honestly, I was actually collecting those lines for a long time that I would say to someone, and it turned out to be you. And I also remember all the cringe, nerdy, and senseless things I did, but definitely I was not that nerdy, it's just I always wanted something to talk to you about.

Hmm... ofc, we never proposed to each other, but the way you replied to my cheesy lines, chatting in the corridor... Seeing everything, I just accepted you as my girlfriend ofcourse. But you had to get ill at the same time, intestinal infection, and you stopped coming to coaching... Took you 2 months to recover. I didn't see you for months then. And then when you were fit, on November 1, I saw heaven again. Talking over chat is easy, but in real life, I just got too nervous, maybe because I saw you after so many days, I didn't know what to do, what to say, and finally... I put my hands forward for shaking hands, and said "Welcome"... that's all. And I guess after that you were never very much interested in coming to the center. And for the next few months, it was really just a few days you visited. Then came class 12th, the initial few weeks you came. Initial days again, we were together, but slowly, we talked less, and after a few days, you even found coming to that place worthless for studying, and preferred home. I did have some other female friends, like really good friends, you did not have to listen to other peeps telling you that one of them was my girlfriend now. And let me be a little honest. What I had actually thought in class 11th was, I will have 1 girlfriend here, then a new one in college, then again a new one after college, ofc, so sometimes I did flirt with others too, but ofc I told all of them that I am yours. And then slowly, there was nothing to talk about, conversations faded, I went to college, you stayed one more year home, no significant texts... Seems like an end of the story right?? You on your way, me on mine. I can pull a new girl now... but I didn't expect that a guy like me would change so much...

What is the sadder part? Just the thought crossed my mind that, ohh if I just continued doing that, there would have been a possibility that I was not talking to you right now... My eyes were suddenly wet. I just never cried for someone else, I never cried, till I met you (At least as a grown-up man).
