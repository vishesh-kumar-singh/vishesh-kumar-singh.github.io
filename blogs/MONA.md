What memories do you have when you were 2 or 3 years old? I have some, and apparently in all of them—the image of my house, my village, anything which comes into my mind—has one thing common in all: **Mona**, a cow. I remember how she looked... I don't know what age I was, but I was just old enough that I was able to walk and speak, when my baba (grandpa) bought her for me, because of course they had wanted to feed me pure cow milk. In villages, children are more free to roam outside. And daily, as soon as baba came home after teaching, I went rushing to him. That day he didn't come riding his bicycle; he was walking with one hand holding the bicycle and another one holding a rope, which was tied to Mona. Oh, apparently, I was just so happy that day... Just in front of my house, a home was made for her. Definitely, I used to drink her milk, but for me she was not ours, she was mine. The little kid believed the cow was bought for him and that she was his own property. I always had to see her, go near her... honestly I don't remember what else I had to do with her, I was barely 3 years old, it is all just a blurry memory, but what I know is she was mine.

---

But guess what, gradually I was 4 and then we had to move to the city for my education. And you won't be taking a cow to the city. Definitely, grandpa and grandma had to stay in the village until grandpa retired from the school, but handling a cow for those two people alone was much of a hassle, so it had to be sold. Definitely I was not smart enough to understand this. It was one of our far relatives only who was buying her; he came to my house. I was playing around, not knowing what was going to happen. But then they told me, he was taking my Mona away. I was held by my aunt at the gate of my house... he went to Mona, untied her, and started moving with her. I cursed him so much, I wouldn't have cursed anyone else in my life up till now. I shouted, cried, and I just said never to come back to my place, don't show your face again, I will take her back, she is mine. And somehow, I never forgot her—the name, the cow, how we met and how she went away, still lives in my memories.

---

But a bigger question terrifies me: what was I to her? I don't know how many times she would have been bought and sold like that. What was I to her? Another one of the humans who just exploit her? Sometimes I put myself in her place and think, she meant a lot to me then, but she casually walked away, didn't struggle much... was it because she had already accepted her fate? Was she already tired of struggling so she just went away?

And it is even more terrifying to think that she may not even be alive at this point. I don't know what would have happened to her body, how would she have been treated when she got old. Where is she now??
